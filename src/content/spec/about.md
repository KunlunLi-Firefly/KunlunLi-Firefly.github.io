# About

I am **Kunlun Li**, a researcher and engineer interested in medical imaging, computer vision, machine learning, and signal processing. My work explores how carefully designed algorithms and data-efficient learning methods can turn complex biomedical and sensory data into useful, reproducible evidence.

Since March 2025, I have worked as an **Application Algorithm Engineer** at **blueballon** in Beijing, China. I completed a Master's degree in Electrical and Computer Engineering at **Johns Hopkins University** in January 2025. From May 2024 through January 2025, I also worked as a research assistant while completing the degree.

My technical path began with signal processing and digital hardware design, then expanded into medical image analysis and data-efficient computer vision. Across these areas, I value interpretable methods, rigorous evaluation, and clear communication about what a result does—and does not—establish.

## Research interests

- Medical image segmentation and quantitative imaging
- Data-efficient and semi-supervised learning
- Multimodal computer vision and depth-aware perception
- Signal processing, speech analysis, and hardware-efficient algorithms

## Selected technical experience

I have worked with MRI and CT processing pipelines, segmentation architectures, self-supervised and semi-supervised learning, digital signal processing, and hardware-oriented optimization. The projects on this site document the research question, methods, evidence, limitations, and my contribution wherever the source material supports those details.

## Working principles

- Keep claims proportional to the available evidence.
- Separate exploratory findings from validated results.
- Make methods and evaluation criteria understandable and reproducible.
- Protect private, clinical, and collaborator information by default.
