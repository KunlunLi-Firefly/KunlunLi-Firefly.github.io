A research portfolio should make it easy to understand the question behind a project, the method used, the available evidence, and the author's contribution. This site is being reorganized around those four elements rather than around a chronological collection of files.

## What is being migrated

The reusable material from the previous website includes project summaries, selected figures, verified publication metadata, methods, and dataset-specific results. Each item is converted into structured content so that it can later be filtered by research area, method, year, or publication status without redesigning the site.

## What is deliberately excluded

Transcripts, examination reports, application essays, and other private records are not part of the public website. A separate, sanitized CV will contain only information suitable for public professional use.

## How claims are handled

Quantitative results are presented with their dataset and evaluation context. Preliminary work is labeled as preliminary, coursework remains identified as coursework, and manuscript status is not upgraded without a verifiable source. This keeps the portfolio useful while avoiding claims that extend beyond the evidence.

The site will continue to evolve as projects, publications, and research notes are added.
